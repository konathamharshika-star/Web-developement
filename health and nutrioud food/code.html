Healthy Recipes

Fresh Vegetable Salad

Approx. 180 calories per serving

Ingredients:

  • Cucumber
  • Tomato
  • Lettuce
  • Lemon juice

Preparation: Chop the vegetables, add lemon juice, and mix well.

Healthy Oatmeal

Approx. 250 calories per serving

Ingredients:

  • Rolled oats
  • Milk
  • Banana
  • Almonds

Preparation: Cook the oats with milk and top with banana and almonds.

Vegetable Soup

Approx. 150 calories per serving

Ingredients:

  • Carrots
  • Beans
  • Tomatoes
  • Vegetable stock

Preparation: Chop vegetables, simmer in stock until soft, and season to taste.

Daily Healthy Meal Plan

  • Breakfast: Oatmeal with banana and nuts.
  • Mid-Morning: An apple or another seasonal fruit.
  • Lunch: Brown rice, dal, vegetables, and curd.
  • Evening Snack: Roasted chickpeas or a handful of nuts.
  • Dinner: Vegetable soup with whole-grain roti and vegetables.

Healthy Eating Tips

  • Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables every day.
  • Choose whole grains such as oats and brown rice.
  • Include protein foods such as beans, lentils, eggs, or nuts.
  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Limit excess salt, added sugar, and highly processed foods.
  • Eat balanced meals and pay attention to hunger and fullness.
